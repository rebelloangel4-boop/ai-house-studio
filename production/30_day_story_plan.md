## Measurement and iteration

Track per episode: impressions, viewed-vs-swiped-away, average percentage viewed, first-3-second retention, completion, replays, comments per 100 views, subscribers gained, and which joke/pacing version was used. Record results at 24h and 7d. Avoid claiming causal lifts without comparable tests. Test one major variable at a time (opening hook, dialogue gaps, visual expression, or ending CTA). At day 7/14/21/30 compare medians, not one viral outlier. Never use copyrighted sound recordings without commercial permission. Keep episodes as review artifacts until approved.

## Locked Episode 2 style guide (approved)

- 1080x1920, 30fps, 24–26 seconds; first frame mid-action, hook within 2 seconds
- Vibrant original 2D vector-flat characters, expressive line art, visual punchlines and reaction cuts
- Luna is the anxious alien friend, Zig the purple deadpan robot, Max the impulsive human, Dino the adorable chaos engine
- Punchy fast voices with minimal dead air, avoid intelligibility loss; sound effects should support the visual action
- Keep bottom 25% free of subtitles and essential graphics; captions higher on screen
- End with A/B viewer choice and a visual callback that can loop to the opening
- Preserve story continuity without claiming an unmeasured poll winner; branch only when votes are available
- Daily Shorts and weekly 16:9 narrative compilations plus separate vertical highlights
- Publishing remains disabled pending approval; monitor first 3 seconds, average viewed percentage, rewatches, votes, follows at 24h and 7d
